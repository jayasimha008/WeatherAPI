1.1.1 2025/01/28
- Built Weather app with city search functionality
- Initally dispalys a basic view of City name, temperature and Icon
- On tapping, it will reveal detailed view with humidity and other details
- On typing in the search bar, the screen is cleared completely
- The last searched city is saved and shown while the app is relaunched
